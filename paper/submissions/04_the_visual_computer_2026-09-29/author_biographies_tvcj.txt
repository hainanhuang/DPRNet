AUTHOR BIOGRAPHIES
==================

Hainan Huang
-------------
Hainan Huang is affiliated with the School of Computer Science, China
University of Geosciences (Wuhan), China. His work focuses on computer vision
and image super-resolution, with particular interest in lightweight neural
networks, efficient visual computing, and content-aware routing. He developed
the Dynamic Prototype Routing method and conducted the implementation,
experiments, analysis, and visualization reported in this study.

Xiang Li
--------
Xiang Li is affiliated with the School of Computer Science, China University
of Geosciences (Wuhan), China. In this study, he contributed to
conceptualization, supervision, resource provision, project administration,
and review of the manuscript. His work on the project focused on developing
and evaluating confidence-aware routing for lightweight image
super-resolution and positioning the method within computer vision and image
processing.
