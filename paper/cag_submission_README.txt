Computers & Graphics - Initial Submission Package
=================================================

Target journal:
  Computers & Graphics (ISSN 0097-8493)
  Do not select the separate journal Computers & Graphics: X.

Submission site:
  https://www.editorialmanager.com/CAG

Article type:
  Research paper

Upload mapping:
  1. Manuscript
     main_cag_anonymous.pdf
     Double-column, line-numbered, anonymized PDF containing all figures and tables.
     The file has 16 pages; references begin on page 13. The cover letter explains
     the small extension beyond the journal's typical 12-page guidance.

  2. Title page
     title_page_cag.pdf
     Use title_page_cag.docx only if the portal requests a Word file. Do not upload both.
     It contains the School of Computer Science / Future City Campus affiliation
     and both authors' institutional email addresses.

  3. Cover letter
     cover_letter_cag.pdf
     This file is anonymized and addresses the journal-specific prior-art requirement.

  4. Highlights
     highlights_cag.docx
     Five highlights, each no longer than 85 characters including spaces.

  5. Declaration of Interest Statement
     declaration_of_interest_cag.docx
     If Editorial Manager supplies its own journal template, use that original
     template, select the no-interest option, and keep the wording consistent.

  6. Generative-AI declaration
     The declaration is already included immediately before the references in the
     manuscript. Upload generative_ai_declaration_cag.docx only if the portal asks
     for a separate declaration file.

Reviewer suggestions:
  reviewer_suggestions_cag.txt is a local reference for entering reviewer fields
  in Editorial Manager. Do not upload it unless the portal explicitly requests a
  reviewer-suggestion attachment. Use the first two candidates by default and keep
  the other two as backups.

Do not upload for the initial submission:
  - LaTeX source files
  - manuscript source ZIP
  - both PDF and DOCX versions of the title page
  - reviewer_suggestions_cag.txt unless explicitly requested
  - the old Neurocomputing or Springer cover letters

Not included:
  - Graphical abstract (optional for the initial submission).

Before final approval in Editorial Manager:
  - Confirm that the journal is Computers & Graphics, not Computers & Graphics: X.
  - Confirm both author emails and the School of Computer Science / Future City
    Campus postal address on the title page.
  - Confirm that both authors approve the CRediT, funding, interest, data, and AI statements.
  - Enter reviewer suggestions from reviewer_suggestions_cag.txt only after one
    final check for coauthorship or collaboration during the last three years and
    any other personal or professional conflict.
  - Review the system-generated submission PDF and confirm that no identifying
    information appears in the manuscript component.
  - Treat the submission as incomplete until the confirmation email is received.

Publishing model:
  Choose the subscription route when Elsevier offers the publishing agreement after
  acceptance. The open-access option is not required for submission or acceptance.

Guide for Authors:
  https://www.elsevier.com/journals/computers-graphics/0097-8493/guide-for-authors
